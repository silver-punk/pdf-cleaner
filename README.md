# PDF Cleaner
A lightweight desktop tool to automatically remove duplicate and blank pages from your PDF files. 100% private, fast, and local.

## Why use this?
* **100% Local & Private:** Your PDFs never leave your computer. No internet required, no data collection. Perfect for sensitive, legal, or financial office documents.
* **100% Free & Open Source:** No annoying paywalls, limits, or hidden monthly subscriptions like online web utilities. 
* **Dead Simple:** Just select your file, click a button, and get a clean PDF in seconds.

## How it works
The app uses file hashing algorithms to scan the internal structure of the PDF, instantly identifying exact duplicate pages and empty/blank sheets without altering the original document's quality.
